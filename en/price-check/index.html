How the total is calculated

  1. Each card is valued at its current Cardmarket reference price in euros.
  2. Quantities are multiplied by that price and added up.
  3. USD and GBP are converted at the fixed rates declared in the build: €1 = $1.08 = £0.85.

The total is a market reference, not an offer. Condition, language and edition can move the real value of a card by more than half. The collection is saved only in this browser.
