How it is calculated

A single sale can move the latest price, so we do not use it. We compare the 7-day average with the 30-day average and discard moves above 60%, which in cards with few sales is noise rather than a trend.

Cardmarket reference prices via TCGdex. Not a prediction or a buying recommendation.
