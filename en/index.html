EUR first, USD and GBP as an approximation

The reference price is in euros because the source, Cardmarket, is the European market. For US and UK readers we add a conversion so the number is readable, but the euro price is the one that comes from the data.

USD and GBP are an approximate conversion at fixed build rates (€1 = $1.08 = £0.85), not live exchange rates.

Prices are reference prices, not an appraisal. A Near Mint copy can sell for far more than a played one; the number does not know the condition of your card.

Why two copies of the same card are not worth the same

  • Condition. Near Mint, Excellent and Played are different markets. The price shown is a reference, not a quote for your copy.
  • Edition and variant. First edition, shadowless, holo and reverse holo can be different cards with different prices.
  • Language. English, Japanese and Spanish copies do not trade at the same price.
  • Reprints. A new printing of a popular character can pull the old version down.

Questions

Are these prices an appraisal?

No. They are Cardmarket reference prices: the average of recent sales. An appraisal requires seeing the card, and condition can change the value by more than half.

Why are USD and GBP approximate?

The source price is in euros. The English pages convert it at fixed rates declared in the build (€1 = $1.08 = £0.85). They are not live exchange rates.

Why only 100 cards in English?

The English edition starts with the 100 most valuable cards. The full catalogue of 9,830 priced cards is available in Spanish.

Looking for the full catalogue?

The Spanish edition covers all 10,648 priced cards, with set pages and the full market radar. Go to ValorCarta en español.
