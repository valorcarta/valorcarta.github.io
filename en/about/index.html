About ValorCarta

ValorCarta is an independent price guide for Pokémon cards. We publish Cardmarket reference prices, not appraisals, and we do not take custody of cards or run a marketplace.

Where the prices come from

Reference prices are daily Cardmarket data collected through the public TCGdex API, the same source used by the Spanish edition. The price shown is a market reference: the average of recent sales, not a quote for a specific card in a specific condition.

EUR, USD and GBP

The source price is in euros. USD and GBP are an approximate conversion at fixed build rates (€1 = $1.08 = £0.85), not live exchange rates.

Amazon affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links use the tag valorcarta-21 and go to Amazon.es. When we have an ASIN for a set we link to that product; when we do not, the link goes to an Amazon search. We do not invent ASINs. The price is set by Amazon, and the commission costs you nothing.

Privacy

No account and no tracking cookies. Your collection and price alerts are stored in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us. We use cookie-less GoatCounter analytics to count visits. If you subscribe to the Spanish alerts service, payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.
